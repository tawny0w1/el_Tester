Drag and drop

Like a Rise matching exercise: drag a piece (left) onto a card (right) and it shows "Matched with Card n". A click without dragging selects a piece, and a click on a matched piece unmatches it. Like Rise, the click the browser fires at the end of a drag is ignored here. The class name matching-interaction-piece is the one Rise uses, so both engines track these pieces.
